    Part one: the papers the AI found and read on its own. Part two: a PDF you give it, for a paper it cannot reach. Each paper becomes a graph of what it says, and the solver judges every link it can.

    If you have access, you can hand the PDF to the AI: it is read like the others, the solver judges its links, and its graph appears here. That part runs on your computer, because a web page has no key and no solver.

        The agent

        Read, propose, verify, repair.

        Language models read well and guess with confidence. Solvers check exactly and cannot read. CellProof gives each the job it is good at and logs every step.
